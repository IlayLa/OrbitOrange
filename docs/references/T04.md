The spherical mean radius and WGS 84 gravitational parameter are used together as a simplified two-body gameplay model, not a full WGS 84 ellipsoid simulation. The 100000 metre atmosphere height is a gameplay cutoff, not a physical claim about the atmosphere's upper edge.

Mission targets, budgets, durations, science requirements, side-quest grants and hazard settings are explicitly fictional placeholder tuning values. No claim is made that their hazard rates or instrument-fit ratings are empirical data. Existing catalogue ratings are read without modification.

Scoring weights and formulas come from the team's plan and T04 contract. The saved challenge documents, team notes and mock-ups are project inputs already recorded in T00. Portraits are original procedural geometry drawn by GeneratePortraits.gd, without external images or real-person likenesses. No external source code, fonts, sounds or additional assets were introduced.
