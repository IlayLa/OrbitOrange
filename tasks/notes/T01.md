## Conventions others rely on
- **Clockwise orbits (for T05).** ω and ν are both measured along the motion, as in GMAT's inverse conversion with inclination 180° (eqs. 4.26–4.27):
  `polar angle = s·(ω + ν)` and `periapsis direction = s·ω`, with `s = +1` counter-clockwise and `−1` clockwise. The apoapsis direction is `s·ω + π`. Example: periapsis drawn at 30°, clockwise motion → ω = 330°.
- **Impact** means crossing `radius + atmosphere_height` inward. A launch starts inside the atmosphere while climbing, and impacts when it comes back down through that radius.
- **Escape** means passing 20 body radii outward on an open path. Escaping segments end there.
- **Pulses at the current angle fire now.** A pulse whose polar angle is within 10⁻⁹ rad of the craft's current direction fires at once, so two pulses placed at one point (staging) fire together.
- **Sampling** is uniform in true anomaly, not in time, so it is smooth near periapsis.
- **`state_at_time` after an impact** returns the impact point (the craft does not move through the planet).

## Deviations from the task file
1. **`tests/test_skeleton.gd` changed (authorised by Ilay).** `_check_plan_resolves` launched horizontally at the T02 stub's 7672.6 m/s, below the surface circular speed (7909.8 m/s). With real physics that starts at apoapsis on the ground and impacts at once, so "one minute after launch the craft is above the surface" failed. The test now sets `plan.launch_angle_radians = deg_to_rad(30.0)`.
2. **`seconds_until_polar_angle` does not return INF for an impact.** Its signature has no body, so it cannot know the ground. It returns INF only for open orbits (direction behind the craft or beyond the asymptote, or any extra revolution) and radial paths. `OrbitModel.build_trajectory` compares it with the new `Kepler.seconds_until_impact`.
3. **Trajectory status comes from how the final segment ends.** For closed orbits this matches "IMPACT when periapsis is inside the atmosphere". An open path launched from the surface has its periapsis inside the atmosphere but escapes, so it is ESCAPE, not IMPACT.
4. **A parabolic orbit (energy exactly 0)** gets semi-major axis −INF, so every open orbit has a negative one, as the contract asks. GMAT itself refuses parabolic orbits.
5. **Radial paths** (zero angular momentum, e.g. zero burnout speed) report IMPACT at once and never reach a pulse angle. The launch-angle slider already forbids vertical launches.
6. Parameters of `launch_state` lost their leading underscore (names unchanged otherwise), since they are now used.
