# D-02 hand-back

- Encounter generation can place a close approach up to 900 simulated seconds ahead, subject to the craft's projected path and the mission deadline. If radar has yet to acquire the contact, the existing fallback can warn within 10 seconds of closest approach.
- Each confirmed debris hit damages the hull and can disable a working instrument. The third hit ends the flight through the existing collision outcome. Each launch starts with a fresh hull. The five instrument-health points follow the existing scoring rules.
- `FlightSimulation.set_telemetry_interference` applies T07's flare hook to live instrument samples. An unprotected flare halves radar acquisition range. A working basic or hardened computer reduces the range loss to 25% or 12.5%, respectively.
- The fuel gauge can raise the passive low-fuel alarm at 25% of attached capacity. The critical 10% alarm still applies to every rocket. The mission timeline records the first early warning and can include altitude and burn-direction evidence.
- The construction catalogue omits the temperature sensor. The flight console covers its retired cutout with the surrounding bezel. The thermal model and overheating alarm have been removed.
- The existing mission timer, science objectives, and side-quest rules govern the flight. Night imaging is a future feature.
- B-11 uses the same next-burn time for the clock and automatic warp slowdown. Orbit insertion appears in the countdown once the departure burn establishes the coast to apoapsis.
- When a threat is active, science credit waits for the collision check. A disabled science instrument contributes through its confirmed hit time. Surviving instruments can still transmit, and a fatal collision cuts off all science credit at impact.
