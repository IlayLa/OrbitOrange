## New field (for T06)
`PlanSummary.blocking_problems: Array[String]`: the sentences of `summary.warnings` that stop a launch. `PlanningConsole.gd` disables Launch from this list, so advice no longer blocks a launch. Advice sentences still start with `"Warning:"` (`RocketModel.WARNING_PREFIX`) for display, but nothing decides by that text.

## Behaviour other tasks should know
- `summary.warnings` = `validate_design(design)` (all blocking) + problems of the plan. Blocking: `"No stage fires at launch."`. `summary.blocking_problems` holds exactly the blocking ones. Advice (starts with `"Warning:"`): the thrust warning, a launch stage that cannot fire or is not on the rocket, `"Pulse N uses a stage that is already dropped or empty."`, and a solid pulse with no direction.
- The thrust warning is made by `summarize_plan`, not `validate_design`: it compares the thrust of the first launch stage that really fires with the mass it lifts at ignition (lower stages already dropped).
- Stages are named by position in sentences: "Stage 1" is `design.stages[0]`.
- `BurnOutcome.mass_before_kilograms` is the mass at ignition, **after** the lower stages are dropped (the mass the engine pushes). When the burn does not fire, both masses are the current mass.
- `apply_burn` with a request of zero or less does nothing and returns `did_fire` false, for solid motors too: a burn with no direction cannot be flown. In `summarize_plan` such a pulse on a live stage counts as affordable.
- A missing `is_jettisoned_by_stage` entry means "still attached" everywhere.
- A weakened engine (`specific_impulse_factor_by_stage` below 1) keeps its rated mass flow and loses thrust, so its burns last longer for the same velocity change.
- `delta_v_from_rocket_equation` returns 0 when the final mass is zero or less, or not below the initial mass.
- A liquid pulse is affordable when it delivers the request to within 0.01 m/s. A solid pulse is always affordable when the motor fires.
- A stage that burns empty is marked dropped at once, so `total_mass_kilograms` no longer counts its dry mass.
- `available_delta_v_meters_per_second` returns 0 for a stage id the design does not have.

## Deviations from the task file
- **"Three tiers per kind"**: every category spans tiers 1 to 3, but not every kind of part has three versions (the fuel gauge and the temperature sensor have one). That keeps the catalogue near the "about 25 parts" asked for.
- **Tanks**: the three large tanks (20000 kg) are `regulated` and the three small ones (1500 kg) are `blowdown`, one of each per material.
- **Sources**: the engine and tank numbers come from named public sources. Costs, sensor noise and update rates, science values, and the mass of some instruments are game values or estimates; each part's `source` says which, and every part has `is_approximate` true. Several engine figures were read from Wikipedia tables that quote the manufacturer, not from the manufacturer's document itself.
- The dry mass of the skeleton parts changed (for example the altimeter from 5 kg to 0.2 kg). Their ids and costs did not.

## Balance (seed mission: 30 million USD, launch loss 1500 m/s, 400 km circular)
**Reaches orbit, 16.5 million USD:**
- Stage 1 (fires at launch): `engine_liquid_basic` + `tank_aluminium_basic`
- Stage 2: `engine_liquid_small` + `tank_aluminium_blowdown`
- Payload: `science_camera_basic`, `telemetry_altimeter_basic`
- Burnout speed 4654 m/s; a launch angle of about 30 degrees above the horizon tops out at 400 km; a 3896 m/s pulse there makes the orbit circular; 4444 m/s stay in reserve.

**Fails, 6 million USD:** `engine_solid_basic` + `science_camera_basic`. Burnout speed about 5900 m/s, below the 8030 m/s a flat launch needs, and nothing is left to circularize, so it falls back.

The remaining 13.5 million USD is the player's room for better instruments, lighter tanks or the hydrogen engine.
