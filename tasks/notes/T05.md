# T05 implementation hand-back

Date: 2026-11-15. User-approved scope supersedes the original launch checkboxes and stage picking.

## Implemented

- Automatic two-manoeuvre ascent to the mission altitude, with an initial thrust/weight feasibility gate.
- Shared sequential mass allocation, including partial liquids, complete solids, liquid-to-solid overshoot, stage separation, and failed-stage blocking.
- Reachability is checked before manual fuel is consumed. Downstream allocations are recomputed after edits.
- Planning and live flight share the allocator. Automatic insertion is distinct from manual pulse indices.
- Read-only stage rows show ascent consumption, fuel left after ascent, and reserve after reachable manual pulses.
- Orbit display includes size-aware altitude mapping, rings, science/hazard bands, launch/craft/threats, stage colours, apsides, and failure warnings.
- Pulse insertion, moving, steering, liquid numeric/slider sizing, deletion, selected-orbit ghosts and immutable drag-start ghosts.
- Ascent and pulse tooltips list contributing stages and their actual fuel/delta-v.
- Standalone demo: res://scenes/development/T05_demo.tscn. Default is the two-stage automatic reference rocket.
- Demo arguments: --scenario=selected, --scenario=impact, --scenario=escape; optional --capture=absolute-file.png saves an agent-QA frame and exits.

## Accepted model assumptions

- Every liquid engine is restartable without ignition limits.
- All orbit-changing manoeuvres, including multi-stage handovers, are instantaneous. Departure duration is played as the existing pre-coast readout; insertion/manual burn durations are readouts and do not delay the impulses.
- Mission loss allowance, two-dimensional spherical gravity, and atmospheric impact boundary remain.
- Normal automatic operation exhausts a stage before separating it. No early-jettison or failed-stage bypass control is included.
- Manual pulse editing begins after the first closed orbit; choosing ignition points during the climb is outside this version.
- A solid that overshoots changes the actual trajectory; no throttle or partial solid burn is fabricated.

## Shared changes authorized by the revised plan

The implementation necessarily changes integrator-owned types, PlanResolver, ExampleFlight, flight execution, the overview's example-rocket entry, and legacy fixtures. These are the prerequisite integration change, not independent T05-only UI edits. RocketModel and Kepler public interfaces remain unchanged.

New interfaces:
- SequentialPropulsion.current_stage, is_solid, apply_burn, base_summary, fill_reserve, stage_number.
- AscentPlanner.resolve and complete_from_departure return AscentResult snapshots.
- PlanResolver.resolve_remaining and resolve_after_ascent compose reachable manual predictions.
- MissionPlan.PlanningMode defaults to AUTOMATIC; legacy_plan() is explicit LEGACY_MANUAL. Duplicate copies the mode.
- PlanSummary.ascent, pulse_allocations, mass_after_ascent_kilograms, mass_after_pulse_kilograms.
- BurnAllocation records each stage outcome, aggregate outcome, pre/post orbital states, and stage_chain().
- TrajectorySegment.source_stage_id and is_automatic_ascent are display metadata. pulse_index remains a manual index.
- OrbitView.show_ghosts, pick_path, stage_color are additions. Its seven existing functions are preserved.
- PulseGeometry converts stretched-screen arrows to/from physical V/B directions.
- ExampleFlight.fill_automatic_flight is the normal-game/demo builder. Existing manual example helpers remain legacy.

## T06 / T07 / T08 handoff

- T06: embed the planning scene and draw chips/back/switch above it. The root ignores blank-area input; view and editor controls consume their own input. Configure overview OrbitView with the complete mission; it scales itself.
- The design mass chip remains launch wet/dry mass. T05's separate mass readout is after ascent or the selected pulse.
- Pulse.stage_id is authoritative only in legacy mode. Automatic pulses express intent and are reassigned after design changes. Do not introduce manual stage selection or delete automatic pulse intent solely because a former supplying stage was removed.
- T07: automatic emergency pulses ignore explicit stage choice and use the current sequential stack. Preview with a copy of vehicle_state through SequentialPropulsion; a pulse starting on a solid is direction-only.
- Flight pulse_fired is emitted once per player/emergency pulse. Automatic departure/insertion have separate event-log records; individual stages are logged with SI quantities.
- T08: live acceleration readouts follow the contributing engines individually; actual fuel is consumed once at the corresponding impulsive event.
