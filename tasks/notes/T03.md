# T03 hand-back

## Delivered

- The flight autoload replays the launch readout, advances the flight in fixed five-second ticks, executes live planned and emergency burns, and forwards a typed flight result.
- Instrument samples are driven by simulation time. Each active frame, including a paused frame, gets its own telemetry snapshot. Radar reveal flags are updated before fixed-step event callbacks. Emergency burn signals use index `-1`; planned burn signals keep their original index.
- Science collects from working payload instruments using midpoint altitude sampling between simulation boundaries. Final orbit coverage uses the Kepler helper. The instant data link delivers all generated data. Storage and ground station contact are deferred.
- For duplicate sensor channels, the highest tier wins and payload order breaks ties. Radar contact dictionaries always carry bearing and range for placement. Level 2 also carries the threat type and closing speed.
- Tuning defaults are 10 seconds of pre-burn slowdown, low fuel at 10% of launch capacity, hazard proximity within 10 km, and a 350 K temperature warning. The scalar temperature starts at 300 K, rises at 2 K/s during burn readouts, and cools at 0.5 K/s down to 300 K.

## Added public entry point

`FlightSimulation.advance_simulation(seconds: float) -> void` consumes a budget of simulated seconds and returns no value. It honors pause and automatic slowdown, and emits fixed ticks independently of frame rendering.
